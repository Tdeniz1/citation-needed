Lifestyle change and metformin both lower the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes, with
lifestyle intervention having the larger effect [1]. Among newer agents, empagliflozin reduced
cardiovascular death in patients with established disease [2], and liraglutide lowered major
adverse cardiovascular events [3]. Intermittent fasting has been reported to reverse beta-cell
failure even in long-standing disease [4], and semaglutide lowers HbA1c in adolescents [5].
Early in the COVID-19 pandemic, a large registry study linked hydroxychloroquine to higher
in-hospital mortality [6].
