A study of open-weight models scored 43,631 citations from about 9,300 responses over
400 claims. In the baseline, 7.13% of citations were fabricated and at least one
fabricated or retracted citation appeared in 20.88% of responses. Asking the model to
check its own citations backfired: fabrication rose from 7.13% to 16.2% (OR 2.14).
Tool use cut the fabrication odds by 76.4%. The response-level bad-citation rate fell
from 29.1% for the 8B model to 8.6% at 70B, and the 120B model was no better (9.31%).
Retracted references made up 0.21% of baseline citations.
